## Decisions the founder should know

* A line the engine cannot buy (gross floor area) is left out of the BOQ by its own words in English
  and Arabic and by an explicit flag. The words are a safety net; the flag is the rule.
* Doors and windows are the only objects whose BOQ code the engine extends. A client BOQ is matched by
  code, so another object type keeps the mapping's code. A client BOQ with one "doors" row now compares
  against several generated rows by size.
* An Arabic BOQ drops an English-only method column instead of printing English in it (the report says
  so, the dimension sheets keep it). Mizan can write Arabic clauses to show it.
* A short-wall deduction no longer stops the method; it is limited to zero and reported as a warning.
  The founder may prefer a hard stop; the choice is one line in `measurement/evaluate.rs`.
