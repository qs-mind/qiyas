# Qiyas scope decision — 20 October 2026

The founder's decision: a general engine with a construction-focused product.

The engine's drawing checks, intent verification and drafting conventions must work on
any drawing: a building, an interior, a machine part or a plane outline. Rules that depend
on a trade belong in optional packs. Construction is on by default in the product; an
automatic drawing check enables it when the drawing has take-off rows or room, space or
area layers. Callers can select packs explicitly.

Construction is the first pack. Interiors and fit-out come next; MEP and structure come
later. We will not add plane-specific or mechanical-specific features, and we will not
market Qiyas as general CAD.

QS Mind's edge is measuring and pricing from drawings. Quanta, Quantix, Tawreed and Qiyas
serve one buyer across that work. Testing the AI in one field gives us repeatable cases
and helps it improve. A general engine supports that focus without building construction
assumptions into every drawing operation.
